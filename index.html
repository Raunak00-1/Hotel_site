1924 — 2026

A Century of Heritage

Founded in 1924 by visionary architect Sir Julian Sterling, Elysia was conceived as a testament to timeless grandeur. Originally built as a private enclave for international diplomats and nobility, the resort has maintained its prestige for over a century.

"To step across the threshold of Elysia is to enter a world where time slows, and every detail whispers of excellence."

From hosting historic peace summits in the 1940s to welcoming modern cultural luminaries, Elysia remains the crown jewel of luxury hospitality.
