Our Legacy

A Century of Refinement

Elysia began as a grand vision in 1924, designed to be the heartbeat of the city's high society. For a century, these walls have witnessed history, hosting icons, thinkers, and seekers of the extraordinary.

Today, we preserve that storied past while embracing a vision of the future. Every column, every velvet drapery, and every warm greeting is an echo of our enduring commitment to the art of hospitality.
